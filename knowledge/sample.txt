The city of Arrakis has no natural rainfall.

Sandworms live beneath the deserts of Arrakis and are attracted to rhythmic vibrations.

The spice melange is found in the sands of Arrakis. Spice is valuable because it extends life and enables prescient abilities.

Fremen wear stillsuits to reclaim moisture from the body in the desert.

House Atreides was ordered by the Emperor to take control of Arrakis from House Harkonnen.

Water is extremely valuable to the Fremen. Fremen communities preserve and reclaim water whenever possible.

Ornithopters are aircraft whose wings move in a manner inspired by birds.

Guild Navigators use spice to safely guide interstellar travel.

Paul Atreides can perceive possible future events after exposure to spice.
